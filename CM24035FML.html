How the system works

The workflow described in the project report, simplified into three stages.

🧹

1. Clean the text

Convert text to lowercase, remove punctuation and numbers, and ignore common stop-words and very short words.

🔢

2. Count useful words

Represent the article as bag-of-words counts and compare word patterns with the six labelled domains.

🎯

3. Predict a domain

Multinomial Naive Bayes combines class priors and smoothed word likelihoods to estimate the most likely category.
